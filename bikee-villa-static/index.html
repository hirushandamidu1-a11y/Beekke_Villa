Our Heritage

Step Back in Time,
Surrounded by Nature

Nestled beside the tranquil waters of the historic Beekke Tank, Beekke Villa offers an authentic slice of Sri Lanka’s rich colonial heritage. A meticulously preserved traditional Walauwa, our villa blends timeless antique architecture—ornate wooden pillars, high wooden ceilings, and sprawling verandahs—with modern luxury and warm island hospitality.

Whether you seek a peaceful escape from the hustle of the city, a romantic getaway, or a unique cultural experience just minutes away from the iconic Galle Fort, Beekke Villa welcomes you to unwind, refresh, and reconnect with nature.

  • ✦ Authentic colonial architecture with original archways
  • ✦ Antique teak furniture & heritage décor throughout
  • ✦ 5-minute drive to Galle Fort & Unawatuna Beach
  • ✦ Fully equipped kitchen & spacious dining hall
  • ✦ Private compound with tropical garden & parking
